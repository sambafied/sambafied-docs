Preferences: General, Storage and Advanced

Guide home · Find a task

Development preview. This guide describes work in progress, not a supported customer release. Follow a procedure only when its controls are available in your build. Availability depends on the server and your permissions.

General​

Review startup and whether the window opens at launch. These are separate settings. Window dimensions and reset are available in some builds; persistence and display fitting are still being checked.

Storage​

Inspect usage, quota and cleanup choices. Unknown usage is not zero. A saved quota does not clear protected work. See storage.

Advanced​

Depending on your build, Advanced can include temporary storage, related-file and pin defaults, Reveal destinations, naming rules, simultaneous transfers, network settings and an optional drive letter. Review the affected connection before changing a value.

A saved preference may need a separate Apply action, reconnect or restart. Inspect the effective result. Ask your administrator for supported proxy and certificate instructions; keep certificate verification enabled.

Reset one sign-in​

Use a connection-specific reset or repair review only when offered. Read the affected account and outcome. Do not clear all browser profiles or unrelated sign-ins. Exact installed reset procedures remain in development.

Change preferences deliberately​

Preferences vary by build. Some changes need Apply, reconnect or restart.

  1. Open Settings or Preferences.
  2. Use General for startup/window, Storage for usage/cleanup and Advanced for connection defaults/maintenance.
  3. Change values and choose Save.
  4. Follow any separate Apply, reconnect or restart instructions. Use Reload saved settings or Cancel draft to abandon unsaved changes.

A saved preference may still be waiting to take effect. Saving a drive letter does not create a mapping.

Keep previous settings if application fails. Do not disable certificate checking or delete all browser sign-ins to repair one connection.
