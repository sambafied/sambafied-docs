Storage, auto-clean and Free up space

Guide home · Coverage and gaps · Image plan

Development guide draft, reviewed 28 October 2026. Steps describe the approved contract and are conditional on the installed build advertising that capability. Product and target acceptance remain separate.

Three different storage actions​

  1. Managed cache clear reviews reconstructable cache copies.
  2. Native Free up space reviews eligible resident originals while leaving the remote asset/placeholder.
  3. Discard Changes intentionally replaces a reviewed local edit and requires recovery protection.

These actions must not share a misleading trash-button promise. Never delete the managed directory to troubleshoot a failed review. Inspect protected and reclaimable bytes, pin state, open handles and the original operation outcome.

Auto-clean policy​

The owner-selected policy is based on modified-date age and exclusions/protection, rather than an unsupported last-access-time heuristic. Choose the available opt-in policy and day count, then inspect its actual applied result. A configured toggle or sample usage number does not prove production cleanup ran. the product team retains policy/native eviction qualification. Quota may constrain new work; it does not authorize destroying protected local changes.

Native Free up space​

Use the exact original-asset native clear review, inspect eligibility and confirm its unchanged generation. Inspect status afterward. A successful managed-copy clear does not establish that Windows resident cache was dehydrated. Dirty, open, pinned and unknown generations stay protected. Failed admission is an actionable refusal, not permission to unregister a root.

W19​

Manage storage safely​

Documentation: FULL. Before you start: Inspect the active connection and distinguish managed copies from native resident data.

Procedure. Open Settings > Storage for usage/quota. Open Advanced for clear, refresh, inventory or relocation reviews. Inspect reclaimable and protected bytes, then approve only the exact eligible review. For relocation, inspect destination and quiescence/copy verification before applying the config switch.

Expected result. Managed cache clear and native Free up space are separate operations. Dirty, open, pinned, pending or unknown generations remain protected.

Cancel, errors and recovery. Cancel before confirmation to retain the prior state. If a clear/move result is uncertain, inspect its original receipt. Never delete cache folders manually as a repair recipe.
