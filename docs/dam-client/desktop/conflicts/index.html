Conflicts, discard and recovery

Guide home · Coverage and gaps · Image plan

Development guide draft, reviewed 28 October 2026. Steps describe the approved contract and are conditional on the installed build advertising that capability. Product and target acceptance remain separate.

Compare before resolving​

Identify whether the remote changed, the local changed, both changed, or the baseline is unknown. Compare original connection, asset identity and generation; timestamps alone are insufficient. Exporting a safe copy can retain work while the remote outcome is investigated.

Recovery custody​

Retained generations and work-folder adoption exist to preserve data after faults, reinstallation or lost observations. Keep the original state and operation receipts. Recovery is not a new import under whichever account happens to be connected. Ask the original-context recovery route to inspect the retained work; do not reset credentials, move private state or discard a file to make an error disappear.

Checkout is an additional signal​

Checkout can be advisory or server-enforced and may be lost or revoked. Do not translate a lock badge into compare-and-swap safety. Unsupported remote protection must stay explicitly unavailable. Use the separate check-in result after reviewing publication.

W20​

Discard local changes with recovery​

Documentation: FULL. Before you start: Be online with a verified remote original and no conflicting writable handle.

Procedure. Select the exact local generation and open Discard Changes. Compare the asset and remote baseline. Review the recovery archive and consent text. Confirm only if the reviewed generation is the one you intend to replace.

Expected result. Recovery bytes are secured before replacement. Discard is not remote deletion, upload or check-in.

Cancel, errors and recovery. Cancel preserves edits. A failed archive, unknown remote state or open writer must block discard. Keep the review and request recovery support instead of deleting the file.

W21​

Resolve a conflict or remote change​

Documentation: FULL. Before you start: Inspect both local and remote identities and observations.

Procedure. Open the conflict record. Compare revisions, sizes and timestamps; Reveal local and Open in Web where available. Prefer keeping an independent copy when uncertain. For use-remote, review the exact generation and recovery protection before consent. For a remote-only change on a clean copy, review Refresh separately.

Expected result. A lock badge, timestamp or Keep Mine label alone cannot authorize overwriting another revision.

Cancel, errors and recovery. If another remote change arrives during review, compare again. Retain unknown/detached edits until a baseline is established; do not force a resolution to clear the badge.
