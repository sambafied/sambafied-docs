Give feedback from DAM Client

  1. In DAM Client, leave the screen or dialog you want to discuss visible. Choose the feedback icon beside Inbox, or open the main menu and choose Give feedback on this screen.
  2. Your default browser opens the DAM Client project. In App context, follow your recent steps to Feedback clicked here. Choose List layout for a chronological list, or Workflow layout to see the steps connected. Returning to the same screen can appear as a separate visit.
  3. Select the image for the step you want to review. The image is a published example of that screen and state. If a dialog was open when you clicked feedback, the app uses the dialog as the endpoint. If a step has no published screenshot, use Screens or Workflows to find a related capture and describe the difference in your comment.
  4. In Feedback, check Context from the app. Add an optional note under What were you trying to do?, or clear Include this app context with my feedback before submitting. Then submit and confirm your review.

What travels with the link?​

The app includes its version and recent navigation in that window, using identifiers for workflows, screens, states and actions such as opening a dialog or going back. A long trail can omit earlier steps. The app keeps account details, AEM addresses, paths, filenames, search text and credentials out of the automatic context. The portal uses published screenshots rather than taking a picture of your desktop.

Included context is visible with your submitted feedback under your GitHub identity. You control the text and images you add. A screenshot you attach yourself can show details that the automatic context excludes.

If the browser or screen does not open​

If the app reports a browser launch error, use its copy or retry option. Paste the copied link into your browser to keep the navigation context. If your installed version has no feedback action, open DAM Client feedback and find the screen manually. Older app links may open a matching screenshot or its workflow directly. If the portal reports invalid or unavailable context, you can still review a screen from the gallery.
