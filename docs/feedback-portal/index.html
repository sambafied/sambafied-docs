Give feedback on the UI you use

Skip the email thread and the Word document. Open the screen you want to talk about, put a pin on the part that needs work, and tell the team what you expected.

Try it on the Transfers screen →

Start where you noticed it

In DAM Client, choose Give feedback on this screen. The deep link brings your recent steps into the portal. Open the published screenshot for the step you want to discuss and choose whether to include those steps with your feedback.

Point to exactly what should change

Suppose you’ve just uploaded a file and want to open its destination folder. Put a pin on the transfer row and write “Let me open the folder from here.” The pin shows where. Your words explain why.

Give everyone the same view

Submit your annotation, then use Link to this review to share the exact screenshot from that round. A teammate can open it, read your submitted comment, and add +1 if the same issue affects them.
