Corporate trials, licences and billing

Request a Corporate trial​

After the company administrator claims the registration, ask your Sambafied contact or partner to arrange a Corporate trial. The trial is provisioned manually with a fixed expiry. It requires no payment and does not automatically become a paid subscription.

Find the licence and activate staff devices​

  1. Sign in to the License Portal as a company administrator.
  2. Open My licences > Company administration and select the company.
  3. Under Company licences, open the Corporate licence to check its status and expiry or reveal its key. Share the key only through your company's approved internal channel.
  4. Staff follow the desktop activation guide. Administrators can use Manage devices on the licence to release a PC activation. Deactivation keeps files and settings on the PC and an offline authorisation may remain valid until its expiry.

Company billing and invoices​

Ready for billing shows that the company has been claimed. Live checkout is currently off.

When billing is available for your company, the partner prepares Checkout and a company administrator completes it with the company's billing details. Open Company administration > Company billing to find paid invoices and the Manage billing option when they are available. If you manage multiple companies, select the correct one first. Each company has its own invoices and licence.

See pricing and Corporate enquiries if you need to discuss a paid licence.
