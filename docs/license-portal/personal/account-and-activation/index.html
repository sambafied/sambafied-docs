Personal accounts, trials and activation

A Personal licence is issued to you as a named individual.

Create your account​

  1. Open the License Portal and select Create an account.
  2. Enter your email and open the verification link from Sambafied. The link expires in 15 minutes. Request a fresh email if it has expired.
  3. Enter your name and choose a password, then return to Sign in.
  4. Open My licences to see the licences assigned to this account.

If you already have an account, sign in with the email you used to register. If you need to recover access, contact your Sambafied representative.

Check your Personal beta trial​

A new verified beta account receives a 14-day Personal trial. If setup shows as pending, sign in again or select Retry trial setup on Overview when it appears. Retrying keeps the original expiry.

For the beta tester reward, use the same account to download the beta from the portal, activate your Personal licence, and submit a testimonial. Track your progress in Rewards. After a Sambafied operator approves the reward, open Rewards to receive a free 12-month Personal licence. See the Sambafied beta programme for details.

Activate the desktop app​

  1. Open My licences and use the copy icon on the Personal licence card. To read the key, choose View licence and Reveal key.
  2. With an internet connection, open About → Manage licence in Sambafied Desktop. Sign in with the same Sambafied account, then paste the key and select Activate.
  3. Check the activation status and expiry shown in the app.

Keep the key private and out of screenshots or support messages. For offline refresh, moving PCs and uninstalling, see the desktop licensing guide.
