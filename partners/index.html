PARTNERS & SUPPORT

Your customer relationships.
Our desktop software.

Bring Sambafied into the workflows you help customers run. Our reseller and support partner model combines your customer knowledge with our product development and technical expertise.

Become a Sambafied partnerHow the partnership works →

01 / SAMBAFIED

We build and maintain the product.

Sambafied develops the app, supplies licences, and provides product updates and technical escalation support under the agreed arrangement. Sambafied retains ownership of its software.

02 / PARTNER

You lead the customer relationship.

Partners can resell licences, help customers get started, and provide first-line support. You handle customer communication, initial troubleshooting, training and rollout within your agreed service scope.

03 / CUSTOMER

A clear place to ask for help.

Customers contact their partner for help with the app. The partner coordinates the response and brings Sambafied in when an issue needs product expertise.

SUPPORT

One customer conversation. Clear escalation.

  1. Report the issue to your partner. Share the app version, what you were trying to do, and the error or unexpected behaviour. Remove passwords, tokens and confidential asset content from anything you share.
  2. The partner investigates. Your partner checks setup, permissions and the connected environment, and helps with usage questions or available workarounds.
  3. Sambafied handles product escalations. When the app needs investigation, the partner supplies reproduction details to Sambafied and keeps you informed. App defects are prioritised under the agreed maintenance terms; issues in connected systems may need the relevant administrator or provider.

Support hours, response targets, covered versions and environments, included assistance, and any additional charges are agreed before service starts. Urgent or after-hours coverage requires a specific arrangement.

LICENSING

Terms that fit the customer.

In a reseller arrangement, the partner purchases licences from Sambafied and invoices the customer. User allowances, licence periods, updates and maintenance are defined in the quote. Customers receive the right to use the app; software ownership stays with Sambafied.

CHANGES

Requests become an agreed scope.

Partners gather requirements and Sambafied assesses whether a request is a defect, configuration task or enhancement. Custom development is quoted separately with delivery estimates and acceptance criteria. Work starts after approval.

General feature suggestions can inform the roadmap without a delivery commitment. Product enhancements remain part of Sambafied unless a separate written agreement specifies otherwise.

COMMERCIAL TERMS

Agree the arrangement together.

Wholesale licence fees, partner margins, payment terms and support responsibilities are agreed individually. Partners price their customer-facing services. Exclusivity, source-code access or ownership transfer would need a separate agreement.
